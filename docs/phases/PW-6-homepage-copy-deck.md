## 10. Copy Principles

1. Plain language before category or technology language.
2. One clear thought per sentence.
3. Positive product meaning before limitations.
4. Operational clarity names concrete objects: klanten, werk, verantwoordelijkheden, voortgang, aandacht.
5. No claim without a PW-1 source.
6. No qualifier detached from its claim.
7. Closed beta early and calm.
8. Sign in / Inloggen is utility.
9. No fake CTA.
10. Course Sellers is relevance, not brand definition.
11. No deferred target-group copy in V1.
12. No standalone AI section.
13. Named controls are not compliance claims.
14. No outcome guarantees.
15. No abstract premium jargon.
16. No artificial urgency or exclusivity.
17. No “we”-claims unless the sentence needs a speaker.
18. No pressuring customer-facing questions as conversion.
19. No stack of negations as the first impression.
20. Mobile shortening keeps product truth.
21. Copy must be understandable without a visual.
22. Internal governance words become ordinary visitor language.
23. Dutch must sound spoken, not like translated SaaS.
24. Copy must be professionally localizable to English later.

---

## 11. Voice and Tone

Use: rustig, helder, direct, competent, menselijk, beheerst, modern, premium without exclusivity, geloofwaardig, not loud.

Avoid: revolutionair; gamechanger; krachtig without meaning; moeiteloos; alles-in-één; transformeer je bedrijf; schaal sneller; groei gegarandeerd; bespaar tijd; werk slimmer; ontdek de toekomst; AI-gedreven without evidenced capability; enterprise-grade; veilig without scope; marketing hyperbole; unnecessary English management terms.
