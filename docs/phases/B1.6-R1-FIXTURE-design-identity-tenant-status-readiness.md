| Concept | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| Lifecycle statuses | `pending`, `active`, `paused`, `completed`, `cancelled` only |
| `archived` | **Not** a status enum value — soft-archive via `archived_at` timestamp + `derived.isArchived` |
| List `?archived=true` | Owner/admin visibility filter |
| Open participation | `pending` \| `active` \| `paused` with `archived_at IS NULL` |
| Terminal | `completed`, `cancelled` (no further status transitions) |

### 8.2 Lifecycle transitions needed for fixtures

| Van | Naar | Allowed | Actor | Workflow | Audit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `active` or `paused` | `completed` | Yes | owner/admin/staff | status + terminal confirm | history + `completed_at` |
| `completed` or `cancelled` | archived (`archived_at` set) | Yes | **owner/admin only** | `/enrollments/[id]/archive` | archive RPC (no status-history row) |
| archived | restored (`archived_at` null) | Yes | owner/admin | `/enrollments/[id]/restore` | restore RPC |
