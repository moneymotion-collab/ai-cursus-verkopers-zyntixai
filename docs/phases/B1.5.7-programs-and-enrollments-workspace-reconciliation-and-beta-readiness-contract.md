## 1. Executive Summary

Programs and Enrollments workspaces are **Git-published through B1.5.6** with seven routes each, role-gated list/create/detail/lifecycle/archive/restore behavior, automated coverage, and authenticated browser evidence for Owner/Staff/Viewer on local production runtime. Metadata UI remains intentionally absent. Progress, Attention, NBA, AI, health score, and churn remain out of scope.

This contract reconciles design, database, application, automated QA, browser, and deployment evidence. It separates **local Git readiness**, **deployment readiness**, **production readiness**, and **Beta-user readiness**. Git publication of B1.5.2–B1.5.6 does **not** prove that commit `7ebf49f` is deployed or production-verified.

Primary residual risks for Beta are: **misleading stale “later phase” copy**, **unverified production deployment**, **Admin live journeys never executed**, **Program open-Enrollment archive block not browser-observed**, and **incomplete Customer/Program contextual integration** relative to the original B1.5 charter.
